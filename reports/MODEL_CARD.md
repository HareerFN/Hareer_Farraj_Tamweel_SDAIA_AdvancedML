# بطاقة النموذج | Model Card
## الحالة
READY_FOR_REVIEW — جودة التفسير تحتاج مراجعة بشرية، وليست درجة آلية.
## الغرض والاستخدام | Purpose and use
This is an educational project on fictional, synthetic Tamweel Lite data. It practises leakage control, forward validation, threshold policy under capacity, calibration and model documentation. A flag (1) means 'send for educational review', not 'reject'. It must not be used for real credit or financing decisions, for scoring real people, for setting prices or limits, or as evidence of fairness for any real region or group.
بيانات Tamweel Lite اصطناعية؛ الهدف حدث خلال 90 يومًا بعد الطلب. 22 خاصية متاحة وقت الطلب؛ لا معرفات أو تواريخ أو هدف في المدخلات. الاستخدام التعليمي فقط؛ لا قرارات تمويل فعلية.
## البيانات والتحقق | Data and validation
حوض التدريب والاختيار: 6576 طلبًا. المعايرة: 836 طلبًا و78 موجبًا. حجز عملاء المعايرة، 90 يومًا لنضج التسميات، وOOF أمامي متداخل مع فصل العملاء في المستويين. طيات المقارنة: 2023Q1 و2023Q3 و2024Q1. نستبعد النتائج التي لم تنضج قبل الأدوار التالية؛ آخر بيانات التدريب لا تستخدم تلقائيًا.
The comparison used nested forward OOF on the 6,576-row fit and selection pool. Each outer fold trains only on earlier, label-mature data, with customers separated in both levels. This gave 2,155 OOF rows across three forward periods. Limits: only 3 folds with about 180 defaults in total, so the SD is descriptive, not a significance test. Warm-up rows have no OOF score. The same OOF rows were used to choose both the model and the threshold, so the results are somewhat optimistic. FAST_MODE used fewer trees.
## النموذج والقرار | Model selection
KEEP SINGLE / Logistic. انحراف AP المرجعي: 0.029808. ارجع إلى artifacts/ensemble_comparison.csv وday5_fold_scores.csv للأرقام الكاملة.
I kept the single Logistic Regression (gate decision: KEEP SINGLE). On three forward folds it had the highest mean AP, 0.392 (fold SD 0.030), with Brier 0.0633 and ECE 0.0188. The best ensemble, Weighted, reached AP 0.389 (SD 0.029, Brier 0.0633, ECE 0.0177), which is 0.002 lower than Logistic. Stack (0.383) and Equal (0.372) were also lower, while XGBoost (0.353) and LightGBM (0.345) were the weakest. No ensemble passed the gate, and the differences are smaller than one fold SD. An ensemble would add three models' complexity for no measured gain, so the simpler, more interpretable model is the better choice.
## المعايرة | Calibration
Sigmoid على عينة محجوزة من التدريب والاختيار؛ الرسم والمقاييس تشخيص على عينة تعلم المعاير، وليسا اختبارًا مستقلاً. لا ادعاء بتحسن على تحدٍّ مجهول التسميات.
The sigmoid was fitted on 836 held-out calibration rows (2024Q3, 78 defaults) that the model never trained on. But the diagnostic is scored on those same calibration rows, so it is not an independent test. On those rows the sigmoid did not help: Brier went from 0.0765 to 0.0781 and ECE from 0.0211 to 0.0349. Logistic Regression was already close to calibrated. ROC-AUC (0.789) and AP (0.288) were unchanged, because calibration keeps the ranking. There are no challenge labels, so no calibration improvement can be claimed on new data.
## السياسة والسعة والمناطق | Policy and regions
خسارة 10 FN + FP، عتبة OOF الخام 0.16892161427109176 والمنقولة 0.12225843144286948. سعة الدفعة 300؛ المرشحون 330؛ الإشارات النهائية 300. كتلة الدرجات المتساوية لا تقسم. 1=إشارة مراجعة تعليمية، 0=عدم رفع الإشارة.
On OOF, the raw threshold 0.1689 flagged 245 of 2,155 requests (11.4%), with a maximum of 11.7% in any period. That gave recall 46.9%, precision 34.3% and a loss of 1,111 units (921 if FN cost = 8, 1,301 if FN cost = 12). On the full challenge batch of 2,500, the transported threshold 0.1223 made 330 requests eligible. The 12% cap allows 300, so 30 lower-scored flags were removed (boundary score 0.1299). Regional false positive rates: eastern 6.3% (n-=507), central 8.0% (n-=489), other 8.1% (n-=494), western 10.3% (n-=486), a gap of about 4 percentage points. Recall ranged from 44.7% to 50.0%. Each region has only 35-49 defaults, there are no intervals, and the data is synthetic, so this is a diagnostic, not a fairness certification.

## المتابعة والقيود | Monitoring and limitations
For each new batch: (1) Drift: compare feature distributions (PSI) for bureau_score, dti, loan_amount_sar and income_sar, plus the score distribution, against training. (2) Capacity: track how many requests pass the threshold versus the 12% cap; this batch had 330 vs 300, so if more than 10% are removed by the cap for two batches, review the threshold. (3) Calibration: once 90-day labels mature, compute Brier, ECE and a reliability curve for each quarter. (4) Region: track false positive rate and recall per region with their denominators, and escalate if the gap grows above about 4 points. Retrain or recalibrate only on new mature data, never on the challenge batch.
OOF يستخدم للاختيار، وثلاث فترات ليست اختبار دلالة. العتبة قد تتغير سعتها عند نقلها إلى نموذج معاد التدريب. لا تسميات للتحدي، ولا مقاييس أداء أو شهادة عدالة له. البيانات لا تمثل أشخاصًا أو مناطق حقيقية.
